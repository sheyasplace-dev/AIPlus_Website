Our company

We are advancing a pipeline of novel small molecule therapeutics, built on multimodal aging-biology datasets and a drug design platform of our own making — backed by a team assembled to push at how biology can be measured, modelled and engineered.

Our work has been published repeatedly across leading journals in biology and medicine, a record we read as evidence the approach holds.

Bold research to unlock small molecule discovery for human health and aging.

Today the mission is age-related disease. The longer ambition is larger: to rewrite the biology of aging itself.
