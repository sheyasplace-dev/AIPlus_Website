What we do
01/03

We are an aging-science company building multimodal biological datasets and AI to discover transformative therapeutics for age-related disease.

We are advancing a portfolio of small molecule therapeutics targeting the pathways that drive age-related decline.

Applying insight from aging biology, our pipeline approaches cardiometabolic disease from new angles.

The integrated platform

Combining aging datasets, chemistry, and AI into an engine of discovery.

Models trained on our own data propose the hypothesis. High-throughput experimental platforms test it against living systems. What comes back — including, and especially, what fails — folds straight into the next generation of datasets, models and molecules.

Our company

Bold research to unlock small molecule discovery for human health and aging.

We are advancing a pipeline of novel small molecule therapeutics, built on multimodal aging-biology datasets and a drug design platform of our own making — backed by a team assembled to push at how biology can be measured, modelled and engineered.

Our work has been published repeatedly across leading journals in biology and medicine, a record we read as evidence the approach holds.

Today the mission is age-related disease. The longer ambition is larger: to rewrite the biology of aging itself.
